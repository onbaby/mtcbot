INSTALL THE REAL BOT

Add MTC Bot to your GroupMe.

Connect your GroupMe account, choose a group you manage, and MTC Bot will begin checking new messages.

  1. 01 Get your GroupMe access token.
  2. 02 Load and choose your group.
  3. 03 Install the bot with one click.

Heads up: GroupMe's public API lets the bot post warnings, but not delete another member's message. Admins still control removal.

Create or copy your token at dev.groupme.com ↗. It is used only to connect the bot and is never saved in your browser.
